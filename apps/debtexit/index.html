From card figures to next steps

Organize and review

Add balances, limits, APRs, minimum payments and confirmed payment dates. Import statement PDFs, images or scans, or update a selected card from a banking screenshot after reviewing the recognized values.

Compare forecasts

Minimum payments are allocated first. Avalanche then prioritizes higher APRs; snowball prioritizes smaller balances. Compare estimated time, interest and a monthly plan around your total budget.

Keep records protected

Financial records are encrypted locally. Optional PIN and biometric unlock on compatible devices help control access. Manual backups use a separate password; keep it independently.

More than a balance list

Track other debts and subscriptions separately, review suggestions for recurring statement charges, and set reminders before known payment dates. Choose English, Russian or Spanish and display amounts in USD, EUR or GBP. Changing the display currency does not convert amounts using exchange rates.

There is no account system or automatic cloud synchronization. Backups include planning settings and financial records, but exclude original statement PDFs/images and PIN or biometric settings. Import replaces saved records and settings rather than merging them.

Privacy policy

Forecasts are estimates based on the figures entered; actual payoff timing and interest depend on bank terms and later transactions. DebtExit does not connect bank accounts or execute payments. Recognition is primarily suited to English-language banking layouts; review all extracted figures. Document scanning requires available Google Play Services.
