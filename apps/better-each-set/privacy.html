Privacy Policy for Better Each Set: Gym Tracker

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Better Each Set: Gym Tracker is developed and operated by Aleksandr Katilov. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Better Each Set: Gym Tracker mobile application.

1. Information You Enter in the App

The app may allow you to enter and store information such as:

  • workout history;
  • exercises, sets, repetitions, and weights;
  • body weight and fitness progress;
  • progress notes;
  • progress photographs;
  • gym preferences and app settings.

This information is used only to provide the app’s workout tracking and progress-monitoring features.

2. Local Data Storage

Your workout information, body measurements, progress photographs, and app settings are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive, collect, upload, or store this information on external servers. Because the data is stored locally, deleting the app or clearing its storage may permanently delete your information.

3. Progress Photographs

The app may allow you to select or take photographs to track your fitness progress. These photographs remain on your device and are not uploaded, shared, or transmitted to the developer. The app may request access to your camera or photographs only when you choose to use a feature that requires this access.

4. Personal Accounts

Better Each Set: Gym Tracker does not currently require users to create an account. The app does not collect usernames, passwords, or account credentials.

5. Advertising

The app does not currently display third-party advertising.

6. Analytics and Tracking

The app does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or behavioral tracking technologies. The developer does not track users across other applications or websites.

7. Sharing of Information

The developer does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. Information may only be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a government authority.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your information is stored locally on your device, you can delete it by:

  • deleting individual records inside the app, where this option is available;
  • clearing the app’s storage through your device settings;
  • uninstalling the app.

The developer cannot remotely recover or delete locally stored data because the developer does not have access to it.

9. Data Security

The app is designed to limit access to your information by storing it locally on your device. However, no device or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including using a screen lock and keeping your operating system updated.

10. Children’s Privacy

Better Each Set: Gym Tracker is not directed toward children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact the developer through the Support page.

11. Health and Fitness Disclaimer

Better Each Set: Gym Tracker is intended only for general fitness tracking and informational purposes. The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or professional healthcare services. Information recorded or displayed in the app should not be considered a substitute for advice from a qualified medical or fitness professional. Consult an appropriate professional before beginning a new exercise program, especially if you have a medical condition, injury, or other health concern.

12. Third-Party Services

The current version of the app does not intentionally send personal or fitness information to third-party services. If third-party services, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, or account features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means that you acknowledge the revised Privacy Policy.
