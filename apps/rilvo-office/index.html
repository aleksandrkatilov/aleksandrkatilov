Word document editor

RWrite

A simple, reliable document editor for Windows. Open regular Word files, edit text, tables and pictures, and save in DOCX for continued work in Word. One toolbar, rulers and paper-like pages keep the workspace familiar.

Tools for your documents

  • Russian and English text editing, undo and redo.
  • Fonts, sizes, bold, italic, underline, text color and highlighting.
  • Alignment, line spacing, indents, bulleted and numbered lists.
  • Normal, Title and Heading 1–3 styles, format painter and clear formatting.
  • Tables with row and column editing, merged and split cells; PNG/JPEG images with resizing; hyperlinks.
  • A4 and Letter pages, portrait and landscape, margins, rulers, headers, footers and page numbers.
  • Printing, PDF export, find and replace, and command search on the toolbar.

S
Excel spreadsheet editor

RSheets

Work with Excel spreadsheets without Microsoft Office. RSheets opens XLSX and XLSM workbooks and XLTX/XLTM templates, calculates formulas and preserves supported formatting and embedded content. It works offline and needs no administrator rights.

From quick lists to larger workbooks

  • 42 popular formula functions, including SUM, IF, VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, ROUND and COUNTIF.
  • English and Russian function names, references between sheets, and multiple worksheets.
  • Fonts, colors, fill, borders, alignment, text wrapping and merged cells; number, date, currency and percentage formats.
  • Sort, filter, find and replace; insert and delete rows and columns with updates to formulas, ranges and objects.
  • Undo and redo, familiar Excel shortcuts that also work with the Russian keyboard layout, and Go To any cell with Ctrl+G.
  • Support for sheets with up to 100,000 rows; can be set as the default app for XLSX files.

  • Preserves VBA macros, images, charts, conditional formatting, data validation and Excel tables.

Works offline. English and Russian interface.

Requires Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. RSheets uses approximately 55 MB of disk space.
