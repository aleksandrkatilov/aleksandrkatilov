Android activity journal

ActivityTracker

Keep a record of the work you have already done. Organize short entries by project, type or dictate an action, and see how your day, week and month add up.

Project information on GitHubExplore features

Android 8.0+. A public APK or Google Play download is not available yet.

From a finished action to a useful history

Capture completed work

Create projects with a name, description, icon and optional text goal. Type an entry or dictate in English or Russian, review the transcript, then save it. Dictation never saves an entry automatically.

See where your effort went

Review daily, weekly and monthly counts, a weekly calendar strip, a date picker and summaries by project. Edit or move entries without changing their timestamp. Archive projects and restore them with their history.

Fit your routine

Choose English or Russian and light, dark or system themes. Set a daily reminder and allow Android notifications. Project goals are descriptive text, not automatically tracked numeric targets.

Start with something you have done

  1. Create a project for your learning, product preparation, video work or another area.
  2. Open it, write or dictate a completed action, and review the text.
  3. Press Save to add the entry to the project's history.
  4. Use Statistics and the calendar to review activity over time.

Each entry is one action. Weeks start on Monday, dates follow your phone's time zone, and times use AM/PM format.

Local records, manual transfer

Projects and entries stay on your device. There is no account, application backend or automatic synchronization. The app does not save audio recordings; Android's installed speech service may use the internet and must support your chosen language.

Settings → Data → Save file exports active and archived projects with their full history as readable, unencrypted JSON, up to 10 MiB. Language, theme and reminder preferences are excluded. Import adds new records and updates matching projects without duplicating the same entries; a conflicting entry cancels the import without changing saved history.

Android automatic app backup is disabled. Keep a current manual export before changing phones or uninstalling. Reminder timing depends on Android permissions and battery management; reopen the app after force-stopping it.
